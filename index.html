Maasik — Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-09-27

Maasik is a period and cycle tracker. This page explains what it stores, where it goes, and how you can remove it. We've tried to keep it plain. If anything here is unclear, write to us at maasiksupport@gmail.com.

The short version

  • Your cycle data is yours. We don't sell it, share it for advertising, or use it to profile you.
  • Maasik has no ads, no analytics and no tracking tools.
  • You can use Maasik without an account. Then nothing leaves your phone.
  • If you sign in, your cycles and settings are backed up so you can get them back on another phone.
  • You can delete your cycles and settings any time from Settings → Delete my data.

What Maasik stores

On your phone (everyone). The dates you log for each period, your usual cycle length, your reminder settings, and the predictions calculated from them. Predictions are worked out on your phone.

In our cloud backup (only if you sign in).

  • Your email address, used to sign you in. If you sign in with Google, we receive your Google account's email address, and Google also shares your name and profile picture with our sign-in provider. Maasik doesn't show or use those two.
  • The start and end date of each period you log.
  • Your usual cycle length, whether reminders are on, the time you chose for them, and your phone's timezone.

We don't ask for your name, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or symptoms.

Reminders

Period reminders are scheduled on your phone itself. They don't go through a server, and we don't collect a notification token. You can turn them off in Settings, or in your phone's notification settings.

Who handles your data

We use a few services to run Maasik. They handle data only to provide that service.

  • Supabase hosts our database and sign-in. Your backed-up data is stored in South Asia (Mumbai).
  • Google, only if you choose "Continue with Google", to confirm who you are.
  • Google (Gmail SMTP), which delivers the sign-in codes to your inbox. It sees your email address and the code.

We don't share your data with anyone else, except if the law requires it.

How it's protected

Data travels between the app and our servers over an encrypted connection. Each account can read and change only its own rows, and that is enforced by the database itself, not just the app.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • Sign out removes your cycles and settings from that phone. Your cloud backup stays, so you can sign back in and restore it.
  • Delete my data (Settings → Privacy & data) permanently deletes your logged cycles and your account, including your sign-in, on your phone and in the cloud backup. This can't be undone.
  • Delete a single cycle from History if you only want to remove one.

If you never sign in, your data lives only on your phone. Deleting it there, or uninstalling Maasik, removes it.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to show you the data we hold about you, correct it, or delete it, and you can withdraw your consent to us keeping it. Write to maasiksupport@gmail.com and we will respond. If you're not satisfied with our answer, you can also complain to the relevant data protection authority. This is the grievance contact for the purposes of Indian law too.

Children

Maasik is meant for people 18 and over.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we'll say so in the app before it takes effect and update the date at the top.
